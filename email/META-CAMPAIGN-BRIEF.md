## Why the Oct 24 revision

The Crown Creative "De Nada Glass Playbook" (July 2026) positions De Nada as
the everyday premium tequila for the modern host, with the tagline "De
Nada. You're Welcome." and Margarita, Paloma and Garden Party as the
signature serves. The playbook has no paid digital or DTC section, so this
campaign is the acquisition engine it does not cover. The copy now leads
with hosting, carries the tagline, and the Old Fashioned (not a signature
serve) is replaced by the Margarita.
