The Story Behind the Brand

Honoring a mother’s legacy

De Nada begins with a simple idea, learned early. Great hosting isn’t about impressing people. It’s about making them feel welcome.

For our co-founder Danny, that came from his mother, Natasha Richardson. An icon of grace and generosity, she brought people together effortlessly. She didn’t entertain to impress. She hosted to connect.

“De Nada exists to carry that legacy forward, to honor it, and to share it with others.”

This is not a celebrity tequila. It is a family story, one about loss, love, and honoring a legacy through the thing she did best: bringing people together.
