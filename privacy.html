The Fine Print

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DNA Spirits, LLC, doing business as De Nada Tequila ("De Nada," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit www.denadatequila.com or our online store at shop.denadatequila.com (together, the "Site"), sign up for our emails, place an order, or otherwise interact with us. It also serves as our notice at collection under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended (the "CCPA").

1. Age Requirement

The Site is intended only for people 21 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 21, and we do not direct the Site to children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from someone under 21, we will delete it. If you believe someone under 21 has given us information, please contact us at orders@denadatequila.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information, from the sources described below.

  • Identifiers and contact details: name, email address, postal and delivery address, phone number, and, for business enquiries, company name. Source: you.
  • Age confirmation: your confirmation that you are 21 or older and, at checkout or delivery, date of birth or identification checks carried out by our retail and delivery partners. Source: you and those partners.
  • Commercial information: products purchased, order history, and gift recipients' names and addresses that you provide. Source: you and our commerce and fulfillment partners.
  • Payment information: card details are entered directly into the checkout and processed by our commerce platform and payment processors. We do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Internet and device activity: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring links, campaign tags in links you click, and interactions with our emails (such as opens and clicks). Source: automatically, through cookies, pixels, and similar technologies.
  • Approximate location: country, state, or city inferred from your IP address or delivery address, used to show where we can lawfully ship. We do not collect precise geolocation.
  • Communications and content: messages you send us, survey or event responses, and content you share with us or tag us in on social media. Source: you and social media platforms.
  • Inferences: limited inferences about your interests (for example, which of our products or recipes you look at) drawn from the information above, used to make our emails more relevant.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information as defined by state privacy laws, and we do not use or disclose any such information for purposes other than those permitted by law.

3. How We Use Information

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver orders, including confirming age and that delivery is lawful in your state;
  • To send transactional messages, such as order confirmations and delivery updates;
  • To send marketing emails, such as recipes, product news, and event invitations, if you sign up for them;
  • To respond to questions, gifting and trade enquiries, and customer service requests;
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Site;
  • To measure and improve our advertising, including on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google;
  • To run events, promotions, and sweepstakes, which will have their own official rules;
  • To prevent fraud, underage purchase attempts, and other unlawful activity; and
  • To comply with law, including tax, alcohol beverage, and recordkeeping obligations, and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

4. How We Disclose Information

We disclose personal information to the following categories of recipients, only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Licensed retailers and delivery carriers that sell, ship, and deliver your order. Every order on the Site is sold and shipped by an independent licensed alcohol retailer in our network (currently through Bevstack), which receives your order and delivery details;
  • Service providers that operate the Site and our business on our behalf under contracts that limit their use of the information, including our e-commerce platform (Shopify), email platform (Klaviyo), website host (Netlify), and analytics provider (Google Analytics);
  • Advertising partners, such as Meta, which receive information about visits to the Site and actions taken on it (such as signing up) through the Meta pixel, to measure and deliver our ads;
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, under duties of confidentiality;
  • Government authorities and others when required by law or legal process, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of De Nada, our customers, or others; and
  • A successor business in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to this Policy.

Shopify. Our online store and checkout are hosted by Shopify, which processes personal information on our behalf to run them. Shopify also offers optional features, such as Shop Pay, that use information about your activity across Shopify merchants; for those features Shopify is responsible for your information under the Shopify Consumer Privacy Policy, and you can exercise rights over it through the Shopify Privacy Portal.

Sale and sharing. We do not sell personal information for money. Allowing Meta and Google to collect information through our pixel and analytics tags for advertising may count as a "sale" or "sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising" under some state laws. You can opt out as described in Section 6. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 21.

California "Shine the Light." We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

5. Cookies, Pixels, and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for three purposes:

  • Essential: to remember your age confirmation and run the cart and checkout;
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the Site. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites; and
  • Advertising and email relevance: the Meta pixel, to measure and target our ads, and Klaviyo, to see which pages email subscribers visit so that our emails are relevant to them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the Site may then not work. You can also limit ads based on your activity through Meta's ad preferences, Google's My Ad Center, and the industry opt-out tools at optout.aboutads.info.

Global Privacy Control. We treat a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser as a request to opt out of sale and sharing for that browser: we do not load the Meta pixel, and we turn off Google advertising features. Do Not Track. There is no common standard for browser "Do Not Track" signals, so we do not respond to them; we do honor GPC as described here.

6. Your Privacy Choices

  • Opt out of sale, sharing, and targeted advertising: turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser (see Section 5), or email orders@denadatequila.com with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share." We will apply an emailed request to the email address and any other identifiers you give us.
  • Marketing emails: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or email us. We will stop marketing emails promptly and keep your address on a suppression list so that we can honor your choice. You will still receive messages about orders you place.
  • Text messages: we do not currently send marketing text messages. If we start, we will ask for your express consent first.

7. Your State Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Virginia, and other states with consumer privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of recipients, and get a copy of it in a portable format;
  • correct inaccurate personal information;
  • delete personal information, subject to legal exceptions (for example, records we must keep under tax and alcohol beverage laws);
  • opt out of the sale of personal information, sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising, targeted advertising, and profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects (we do not engage in such profiling); and
  • not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

How to make a request. Email orders@denadatequila.com with the subject "Privacy Request," or write to us at the address in Section 13. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days, which we may extend by another 45 days where the law allows, with notice. To protect you, we verify requests by matching information you give us (such as your email address and an order number) with our records. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask the agent for your signed permission and ask you to verify your identity directly.

Appeals. If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject "Privacy Appeal." We will respond within the time the law requires and explain our reasons. If your appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general.

Nevada residents may ask us not to sell covered information by emailing the address above; we do not sell covered information as Nevada law defines it.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes in this Policy, using these criteria:

  • Order and transaction records: for as long as tax, accounting, and alcohol beverage laws require, generally up to seven years after the order;
  • Email list: while you are subscribed, and for up to three years after your last interaction with our emails if you have not unsubscribed. If you unsubscribe, we keep your address on a suppression list so that we do not email you again;
  • Enquiries and customer service messages: up to three years after the conversation ends;
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months in Google Analytics; and
  • Information needed for legal claims or required by law: for as long as that need or requirement lasts.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, and our commerce providers encrypt payment information in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as the law requires.

10. Third-Party Sites

The Site links to sites we do not control, including social media platforms and press coverage. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle information.

11. Visitors Outside the United States

The Site is intended for people in the United States, and we ship only to U.S. addresses. Information we collect is processed and stored in the United States.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and, if the changes are material, give additional notice, such as a notice on the Site or an email. Changes apply from the date they are posted.

13. Contact Us
