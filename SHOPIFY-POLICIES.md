# Shopify policy pages, ready to paste

Shopify → Settings → Policies. The goal is that a customer reads the same
thing on shop.denadatequila.com, on denadatequila.com/shipping.html and in
the emails. The Terms of Service page in Shopify is accurate; the notes below
fix three loose ends in it and give you a Shipping policy and a Refund policy
that match the site.

## Three fixes in the existing Terms of Service

1. **"a $ restocking fee"** under *Failure to Deliver* has no amount. Fill it
   in (ask the retailer what they charge) or say "the retailer's restocking
   fee".
2. **"within a certain amount of days"** under *Refunds and Exchanges* is
   placeholder text. Use "within 5 to 10 business days" or whatever the
   retailer commits to.
3. **The *Refunds and Exchanges* section contradicts the section above it.**
   *Missing, Incorrect, or Damaged Goods* says returns cannot be accepted and
   photos are the proof. *Refunds and Exchanges* then talks about the returned
   item being "received and inspected". Pick one. The site follows the first
   (photos, nothing shipped back), because it is what the law allows and what
   the customer prefers. Replace the *Refunds and Exchanges* section with the
   refund policy below.

## Refund policy (paste into Settings → Policies → Refund policy)

All sales are final. Because of U.S. state laws on shipping alcohol, we cannot
accept returns or exchanges, opened or unopened.

If your order arrives damaged, defective, incomplete or incorrect, email
orders@denadatequila.com within 3 days of delivery with your order number and
clear photos or a short video of the bottle and the box, and tell us whether
you would like a replacement or a refund. We review the proof and confirm by
email. An approved refund is credited to your original method of payment
within [5 to 10] business days. You do not need to ship anything back.

If you enter an incorrect delivery address or nobody over 21 is available to
sign, the carrier returns the package to the retailer. Email
orders@denadatequila.com as soon as you notice. A returned order is refunded
for the cost of the bottles, less shipping and a [$X] restocking fee.

If the retailer is unable to fulfil an order, or a pricing or listing error
occurs, the order is cancelled and your card is credited in full.

Questions: orders@denadatequila.com.

## Shipping policy (paste into Settings → Policies → Shipping policy)

Every order is fulfilled and shipped by a licensed liquor retailer in our
network, not by De Nada Tequila. An order is accepted when the retailer ships
it. The charge appears on your statement as BEVSTACK-WINE/LIQR.

We ship to most U.S. states. Checkout confirms your address before you pay.
We cannot deliver to PO boxes, freight forwarders or parcel lockers.

An adult (21+) signature is required on delivery, and the carrier may ask for
government-issued ID. The package will not be left unattended. If nobody is
available, the carrier attempts delivery again on a later business day; if the
package is returned to the retailer, our refund policy applies.

Orders usually leave the retailer within [1 to 2] business days and arrive in
[2 to 5] business days. Shipping cost is shown at checkout. In extreme heat or
cold the retailer may hold a shipment briefly to protect the bottle. Holiday
cut-off dates are announced by email.

By ordering you confirm you are 21 or over, that the bottle is for your own
use or a gift to someone 21 or over in the U.S., that your state and the
delivery state permit the sale and delivery of alcohol, and that you will not
resell it.

Questions: orders@denadatequila.com.
