# Photo placement plan (Rebeca's dinner + gold-curtain shoot, Sept 2026)

About 145 photos reviewed. Filenames were not preserved in review, so each
pick is described by what is in the frame. Find it in the "De Nada small"
folder, then send it over with its DSCF number and it goes in.

Two shoots in the folder: the **dinner** (kitchen prep, steel bar table,
candlelit table, aftermath) and the **gold curtain** portraits. Plus two
headshot sets on wood panelling. Everything is warm, flash-lit, a little
loose. Exactly the brand.

## Done (Sept 22, revised)

`-800.webp` (site) and `img/email/<name>.jpg` (Klaviyo, 1200 px). Adam's

photo earns its place only where the copy is about people, a place or a
bottle, and nothing else on the page is already doing that job. Recipes keep
their cocktail renders on the site and in every email (the renders are also
exported to `img/email/cocktails/<slug>.jpg`). Nudges (abandoned checkout,
