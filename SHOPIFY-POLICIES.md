## Refund policy

Effective November 24, 2026. The same terms appear in sections 6 and 7 of our
Terms of Use at https://www.denadatequila.com/terms.html.

**All sales are final**
Every order is sold and shipped by an independent licensed alcohol retailer in
our network. Because state laws restrict the return shipment of alcohol, we
cannot accept returns or exchanges, opened or unopened.

**Damaged, missing, or incorrect items**
If your order arrives damaged, defective, incomplete, or incorrect, email
orders@denadatequila.com within 3 days of delivery with your order number and
clear photos or video of the product and packaging. We will review it and
reply by email. If approved, you will receive a replacement or a refund to
your original payment method, as you prefer. You do not need to send anything
back.

**Failed delivery**
Delivery requires the signature of an adult aged 21 or older. If you give an
incorrect address, or no adult is available to sign after the carrier's
delivery attempts, the package may be returned to the retailer. Please email
orders@denadatequila.com as soon as possible. When a returned package reaches
the retailer, you will be refunded the price of the products, less the
original shipping charge and a $20 restocking fee.

**Cancellations and errors**
To change or cancel an order, email us with the order number as soon as you
can. If the retailer has not shipped it yet, we will do our best to stop it.
If an order is declined or cancelled by the retailer, or cancelled because of
a pricing or listing error, you receive a full refund.

**Refund timing**
Approved refunds are issued promptly to your original payment method. Your
bank or card issuer may take a few days to show the credit.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under applicable law that
cannot be waived.
