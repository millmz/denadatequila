Make it more than a bottle

Small touches that turn a bottle into a gift.

The margarita kit

Blanco, a bag of limes, a small bottle of agave nectar and the recipe on a card: 2 oz De Nada Blanco, 1 oz fresh lime, ½ oz agave. Shake, pour over ice.

The tasting flight

All three bottles and a note: “Taste them young to old.” Our tasting guide tells them how.

The after-dinner pour

Añejo with a bar of good dark chocolate. The chocolate notes in the tequila do the rest.

Gift questions

Is tequila a good host gift?

Yes. Most host gifts go on a shelf; a good bottle of tequila usually gets opened that evening, with the person who brought it. Pick a blanco for a host who makes drinks and an añejo for one who sips.

What is a good tequila gift under $60?

De Nada Blanco ($49.99) or Reposado ($54.99), both single-estate tequilas from NOM 1414 in the highlands of Jalisco. The Reposado was named Robb Report’s best reposado for cocktails in 2026.

What is a good tequila gift for someone who has everything?

A sipping añejo. De Nada Añejo ($79.99) rests more than twelve months in ex-bourbon oak, with chocolate and baking spice. Or give all three expressions so they can taste the difference oak makes.

Can I ship tequila as a gift?

Yes, to most U.S. states. Orders on denadatequila.com are shipped by licensed retailers, an adult 21+ must sign for the box, and shipping is free on three or more bottles. Send it to an address where someone will be in during the day.

When should I order tequila for the holidays?

Orders usually leave the retailer within one to two business days and arrive two to five business days after that. Order by early December to be safe; this year’s holiday cut-off dates go out by email.
