The Fine Print

Accessibility

Last updated: November 2026

We want everyone of legal drinking age to be able to find, read and order from De Nada Tequila, whatever device or assistive technology they use. This statement covers www.denadatequila.com and our online store at shop.denadatequila.com.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. We check the site with automated testing and by hand, including keyboard-only use, and we review new pages before they go live.

What we have done

  • Text and buttons meet WCAG contrast ratios against their backgrounds.
  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline, and is marked up with landmarks so screen readers can jump straight to the main content.
  • The age check and the email sign-up keep keyboard focus inside them while they are open, and the sign-up closes with the Escape key.
  • Images carry text descriptions, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Moving content can be paused, and animation is switched off for visitors who ask their device to reduce motion.
  • Pages use headings, landmarks and labelled form fields so screen readers can navigate them.

Known limits

Checkout runs on Shopify's hosted checkout, and some payment buttons (Shop Pay, Google Pay, PayPal) are provided by those companies. Instagram content we link to is hosted by Instagram. We cannot change those pages directly, but we will help you complete an order another way if they get in your way.

Tell us about a problem

If anything on the site is hard to use, or you would like information in another format, please tell us and we will help you directly, including taking your order by phone or email:

  • Email: orders@denadatequila.com (subject line "Accessibility")
  • Phone or text: 305-962-2509
  • Mail: De Nada Tequila (DNA Spirits, LLC), 148 South Liberty Drive, Stony Point, NY 10980

We aim to reply within two business days.
