Tommy’s Margarita

The modern classic, De Nada style Made with De Nada Reposado →

Ingredients

  • 2 oz De Nada Reposado
  • 1 oz Fresh Lime Juice
  • ½ oz Agave Nectar

Method

Combine all ingredients in a shaker with plenty of ice; shake hard for 15 seconds. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice and garnish with a lime wedge. Salt rim strictly optional; good tequila doesn’t need the mask. No orange liqueur, no fuss. The Reposado does the talking.

Tequila Sunrise

the classic, done properly Made with De Nada Blanco →

Ingredients

  • 2 oz De Nada Blanco
  • 1 oz Fresh Lime Juice
  • 3 oz Orange Juice
  • ½ oz Agave
  • Garnish: Maraschino Cherries & Juice

Method

Fill a tall glass with ice. Add tequila, lime juice, orange juice, and agave. Stir gently to combine. Slowly pour maraschino cherry juice down the side of the glass to create a sunrise effect. Garnish with maraschino cherries.

Espresso Martini

the Reposado twist on a modern classic Made with De Nada Reposado →

Ingredients

  • 1.5 oz De Nada Reposado
  • 1 oz Fresh Espresso, Chilled
  • ½ oz Coffee Liqueur
  • ½ oz Agave Nectar
  • Garnish: 3 Coffee Beans

Method

Combine all ingredients in a shaker. Add ice and shake hard for 10 seconds. Double strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with three coffee beans.

Muchas Gracias

by Jillian Vose
Tiki Mug · Pineapple Leaf, Grated Nutmeg
The Recipe
  • 1.5 oz De Nada Reposado
  • ½ oz Dolin Génépy Liqueur
  • ½ oz Fresh Pineapple Juice
  • ½ oz Fresh Lime Juice
  • ½ oz Fennel Seed Syrup
  • ½ oz Macadamia Nut Syrup
  • ¼ oz Heavy Cream
  • 1 heaping tsp Matcha–Coco López Mix

Combine all ingredients in a shaker tin with three cubes of ice; hard shake briefly. Strain into a tiki mug over pebble ice, topping with more as needed. Garnish with a pineapple leaf and freshly grated nutmeg.

Smoke & Spice Margarita

by Anthony Baker
Short Glass · Sage Leaf, Jalapeño Slice
The Recipe
  • 1.5 oz De Nada Reposado
  • ½ oz Mezcal
  • 1 oz Fresh Lime Juice
  • ½ oz Agave Nectar
  • 1 Jalapeño Slice
  • Pinch of Salt

Muddle the jalapeño in a shaker, then add the agave, lime, mezcal, tequila, and ice. Shake for ten seconds and strain into a short glass over ice. Sprinkle a pinch of salt on top, rub the rim with a sage leaf, and garnish with the sage and a jalapeño slice.
