The story

De Nada began with a simple idea, learned early: great hosting isn’t about impressing people. It’s about making them feel welcome. The name is Spanish shorthand for “you’re welcome,” and the brand is built around it.

The first bottles were filled in the fall of 2020. By January 2021 the founders were selling them door to door in New York, before any distributor would take them. In 2024 De Nada put tequila in the world’s first aluminum tequila bottle. In 2026 it moved to premium recycled glass and added an Añejo.

The founders

Danny Neeson, co-founder and president. Danny started De Nada to honor his mother, Natasha Richardson, and her gift for making people feel at home. Before De Nada he founded Pine Outfitters, an activewear line made from recycled plastic.

Adam Millman, co-founder and CEO. Adam leads the business and the brand. The two met at Tulane University in New Orleans and have been friends since.
